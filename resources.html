Common Development Questions

When should I start the permitting process?

Ideally, permitting discussions should begin during site selection. Understanding approval timelines early can prevent costly delays and inform your pro forma assumptions.

What's the difference between a feasibility study and due diligence?

Feasibility is a high-level "go/no-go" assessment. Due diligence is deeper investigation once you're under contract. Both are critical, but feasibility should happen first to avoid wasting time on non-viable sites.

How do I know if my consultant team has scope gaps?

Common gaps include: civil not coordinating with MEP on utility service, architect not accounting for grease trap requirements, or no one tracking municipal approval dependencies. A coordination consultant can identify these early.
